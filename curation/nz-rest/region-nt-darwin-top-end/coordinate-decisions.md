No pin is in water. Every flag is a decision to keep the visitor point over a reference's centroid:

- darwin-14 Berry Springs (2.5 km from Wikipedia): Wikipedia's point is the locality; the pin is OSM leisure=park "Berry Springs Nature Park".
- darwin-16 Casuarina Coastal Reserve (4.5 km Wikipedia, 0.8 km OSM centroid): a long coastal reserve, pinned at its main beach access (Dripstone Beach picnic site), not a centroid.
- darwin-17 Charles Darwin National Park (1.4 km Wikipedia/OSM centroid): pinned at an information board in the park's visitor entrance area.
- darwin-18 Fogg Dam (392 m Wikipedia, 2.0 km OSM reserve centroid): pinned on the dam-wall viewpoint (agrees with the Commons photo median).
- darwin-20 Palmerston (161 m Wikipedia): OSM place=town node (town centre).
- litchfield-2 Florence Falls (324 m), litchfield-3 Buley Rockhole (105 m), litchfield-4 Termite Mounds (118 m from the car park): two sources agreed at pin resolution; the points are the falls, the rockhole and the mounds' boardwalk.
- litchfield-6 Batchelor (2.5 km Wikipedia): OSM place=town node.
- litchfield-7 Tabletop Track (9.9 km from one OSM path segment): a 39.6 km walk pinned at the middle of its chained OSM ways by design (COVERAGE.md); OSM has no route relation, so the order of the chain is approximate.
