# South Australia: coverage regions (run curation/sa)

The whole state is split into ten regions, following the South Australian Tourism Commission's eleven tourism regions
(Adelaide, Adelaide Hills, Barossa, Clare Valley, Fleurieu Peninsula, Kangaroo Island, Murray River Lakes & Coorong,
Limestone Coast, Yorke Peninsula, Eyre Peninsula, Flinders Ranges & Outback) with Adelaide and the Adelaide Hills as one
region, the Barossa and the Clare Valley (with the Mid North) as one, and the Flinders Ranges & Outback split into the
Flinders Ranges and the Outback. Each region pass covers ALL of its land: towns, parks, coast, islands, outback and the
roads between them. A place on a boundary belongs to the region it lies in by the lines below; if unsure, include it and
say so in its `notes` (the proposer resolves duplicates). Timezone everywhere: Australia/Adelaide.

- **sa-adelaide — Adelaide & Adelaide Hills.** Greater Adelaide: the city and North Adelaide, the Park Lands, North
  Terrace's institutions, the Adelaide Central Market, the beaches and seaside suburbs from Sellicks Hill's north
  (Aldinga is Fleurieu) to St Kilda (Glenelg, Brighton, Henley Beach, Grange, Semaphore, Largs Bay, Hallett Cove),
  Port Adelaide, Morialta, Belair National Park, Cleland and Mount Lofty, the Waterfall Gully track, Adelaide's
  suburbs and the northern plains to Gawler (exclusive: Gawler is sa-barossa-clare); and the Adelaide Hills: Hahndorf,
  Stirling, Crafers, Mount Barker, Birdwood, Lobethal, Woodside, Gumeracha, Mount Pleasant, Uraidla, Mylor, Echunga,
  Meadows, Kuitpo Forest, Mount Barker summit, the Torrens Gorge, and the Hills' parks and wineries.
- **sa-barossa-clare — Barossa, Clare Valley & Mid North.** Gawler, the Barossa Valley (Tanunda, Nuriootpa, Angaston,
  Lyndoch, Bethany, Seppeltsfield, Marananga), the Eden Valley and Springton, Kapunda, the Clare Valley (Clare, Sevenhill,
  Auburn, Watervale, Penwortham, Mintaro and Martindale Hall, the Riesling Trail), Burra, Riverton, Saddleworth, Balaklava,
  Snowtown, Blyth, Jamestown, Spalding, Hallett and the Mid North wheatbelt south of Peterborough.
- **sa-fleurieu — Fleurieu Peninsula.** McLaren Vale and its wine region, Willunga, Onkaparinga River National Park and
  Gorge, Maslin Beach, Port Willunga, Aldinga Beach and Aldinga Scrub, Sellicks, Myponga, Normanville, Carrickalinga,
  Second Valley, Rapid Bay, Cape Jervis, Deep Creek National Park, the Heysen Trail's south end, Victor Harbor, Granite
  Island, Encounter Bay, Port Elliot, Middleton, Goolwa and the Murray Mouth's Hindmarsh Island, Strathalbyn, Langhorne
  Creek, Currency Creek, Mount Compass, Yankalilla, Newland Head, Waitpinga.
- **sa-kangaroo-island — Kangaroo Island.** The whole island: Penneshaw, Kingscote, American River, Emu Bay, Vivonne
  Bay, Flinders Chase National Park (Remarkable Rocks, Admirals Arch, Cape du Couedic), Seal Bay, Little Sahara, Kelly
  Hill Caves, Stokes Bay, Snelling Beach, Western River, Cape Willoughby, Cape Borda, Lathami, Pennington Bay, the
  island's wineries and producers.
- **sa-murray-coorong — Murray River, Lakes & Coorong.** The Riverland (Renmark, Berri, Loxton, Barmera and Lake
  Bonney, Waikerie, Morgan, Paringa, Monash, the Murray River National Park, Chowilla, Calperum and the Bookmark
  biosphere), the Murraylands (Murray Bridge, Mannum, Swan Reach, Blanchetown, Purnong, Tailem Bend, Monarto Safari
  Park, Karoonda, Pinnaroo), the Lower Lakes (Lake Alexandrina, Lake Albert, Milang, Clayton Bay, Wellington,
  Narrung, Meningie, Raukkan) and the WHOLE of Coorong National Park (to Salt Creek and the 42 Mile Crossing).
- **sa-limestone-coast — Limestone Coast.** Kingston SE (the Big Lobster), Cape Jaffa, Robe, Beachport, Southend,
  Millicent, Tantanoola, Mount Gambier (Blue Lake, Umpherston Sinkhole, Cave Garden), Port MacDonnell, Mount Schank,
  Piccaninnie Ponds, Ewens Ponds, Penola and Coonawarra, Naracoorte and Naracoorte Caves, Bool Lagoon, Lucindale,
  Padthaway, Keith, Bordertown, Mundulla, Canunda, Little Dip and the coastal parks.
- **sa-yorke — Yorke Peninsula.** The Copper Coast (Kadina, Wallaroo, Moonta, Moonta Bay, Port Hughes), Port Broughton,
  Port Victoria and Wardang Island, Maitland, Ardrossan, Port Vincent, Stansbury, Edithburgh, Yorketown, Minlaton,
  Warooka, Point Turton, Marion Bay, Dhilba Guuranda-Innes National Park (Inneston, Ethel wreck, West Cape), Corny
  Point, Point Souttar, Troubridge Island, the Walk the Yorke trail.
- **sa-eyre — Eyre Peninsula & Far West Coast.** Whyalla, Cowell, Franklin Harbour, Arno Bay, Port Neill, Tumby Bay
  and the Sir Joseph Banks Group, Port Lincoln (Boston Bay, Tunarama country), Lincoln National Park, Memory Cove,
  Whalers Way, Coffin Bay and its national park, Mount Dutton Bay, Elliston, Talia Caves, Venus Bay, Baird Bay,
  Point Labatt, Streaky Bay, Murphy's Haystacks, Smoky Bay, Ceduna, Thevenard, Fowlers Bay, Penong and its windmills,
  Cactus Beach, the Nullarbor (Head of Bight, Bunda Cliffs, Nullarbor National Park, Nullarbor Roadhouse, Border
  Village), the inland Eyre (Kimba and the Big Galah, Wudinna, Mount Wudinna, Minnipa and Pildappa Rock, Gawler Ranges
  National Park, Lake Gairdner, Iron Knob).
- **sa-flinders — Flinders Ranges.** Port Augusta (Australian Arid Lands Botanic Garden, Wadlata), Port Pirie, Port
  Germein, Crystal Brook, Laura, Gladstone, Wirrabara, Melrose and Mount Remarkable, Mount Remarkable National Park
  (Alligator Gorge, Mambray Creek), Wilmington, Quorn and the Pichi Richi Railway, Warren Gorge, Hawker, Kanyaka,
  Peterborough, Orroroo, Carrieton, Yunta, Ikara-Flinders Ranges National Park (Wilpena Pound, Brachina and
  Bunyeroo Gorges, Rawnsley Bluff, Arkaroo Rock), Rawnsley Park, Blinman, Parachilna, Angorichina, Chambers Gorge,
  Arkaroola, Vulkathunha-Gammon Ranges National Park, Nepabunna, Iga Warta, Leigh Creek, Lyndhurst, Copley,
  Beltana.
- **sa-outback — Outback.** North of the Flinders Ranges and the Eyre: Marree, Lake Eyre / Kati Thanda, the
  Oodnadatta Track (Farina, Coward Springs, Wabma Kadarbu Mound Springs, William Creek, Oodnadatta, the Pink
  Roadhouse), Anna Creek, Lake Eyre South, the Birdsville and Strzelecki Tracks, Innamincka and Cooper Creek, the
  Burke and Wills sites, the Dig Tree's SA side, Coongie Lakes, Coober Pedy (dugouts, mines, Breakaways, the Dog
  Fence, Moon Plain), the Painted Desert (Arckaringa), Lake Cadibarrawirracanna, Woomera, Roxby Downs, Andamooka, Lake
  Torrens' edge, Pimba, Glendambo, Kingoonya, Lake Hart, Marla, Mintabie, the Simpson Desert's SA side (Witjira
  National Park, Dalhousie Springs, Purnie Bore), the APY Lands' visitor sites only where a source names one open to
  visitors.
