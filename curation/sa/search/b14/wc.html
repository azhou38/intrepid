Welcome to the sa whale Centre

Discover the beauty of the deep

Set within the stunning historic Railway Goods Shed on Railway Terrace, Victor Harbor. The South Australian Whale Centre adorns, educates and inspires visitors to protect and embrace the local marine environment. 

Discover the giants of the deep at the SA Whale Centre today. 

Exhibitions

in the South Australian Whale Centre
Rodney Fox Shark Exhibit

Jump into the cage to take a dive and snap with a Great White Shark and learn about these creatures of the deep.

Southern Right Whale Mural

Look in awe as you see the lifesize mural of resident whales Buttons and Bobby who called the Encounter Marine Park home in 2022.

Discover the wall of whales

Below the surface of the ocean discover the many whales that call our oceans home.

Fossil Dig

Delve below the earths surface to discover the hidden fossils in our fossil dig.

Acknowledgment Of Country
The South Australian Whale Cente acknowledge that the Ngarrindjeri and Ramindjeri people are the Traditional Owners of our beautiful lands and surrounding waters. We recognise and respect the cultural connections the past, present and future Traditional Custodians have. We are committed to working with the Ngarrindjeri and Ramindjeri people, supporting their role as custodians of their lands and helping ensure the future prosperity and cultural enrichment of the community.
