Magill Estate Restaurant wins Two MICHELIN Stars

14 OCT 2026

This 180-year-old winery just became home to the most talked-about table in the country.

Picture the high-end kitchen you've seen on TV time and time again: one man at the pass, sweating and swearing his way through the shift, his name the only one above the door. That's the expectation of an award-winning restaurant, but it's not what you'll find at Magill Estate Restaurant. This kitchen has just been awarded Two MICHELIN Stars, the highest count ever awarded in Australia, and executive chef Scott Huggins won't let the story start and end with him. "Being one of the first restaurants in Australia to get a MICHELIN Star is the most rewarding thing," he says, "not just for myself, but more so for my team."

Everyone works hard. That includes the dishie who's three sinks deep at midnight, the apprentice boning quail before the sun's up and the gardener out picking herbs in their estate garden while the dining room's still asleep. Scott wants the credit split as many ways as there are hands in the kitchen. It's the same principle behind every bottle next door, from one of the most recognised wine names on earth, founded on this exact site in 1844. Its blends are never one vineyard's work. Penfolds' winemakers taste hundreds of parcels a vintage, pulling fruit from Barossa, McLaren Vale, sometimes further, and blend in only what the wine actually needs. 

Visit Magill Estate Restaurant

A wine history

The vines you can see from the cellar door and restaurant's window are some of the oldest in the country. Just 15 minutes from Adelaide's CBD, when you walk past the old underground barrel rooms it still feels like another decade entirely. That history sits behind every pairing on the menu. With one of the country's deepest back-vintage cellars on hand, Scott isn't limited to what's currently in bottle. "We've got a unique opportunity to create food around wine, which is one of our biggest things," he says. "Sometimes there's an amazing wine we want to showcase and we'll create a dish around that. Sometimes we'll create a dish because the ingredients are amazing. With such an extensive cellar, we're lucky we can choose a wine that will work with any dish."

It's all I know how to do...I think cooking is the one thing that brings me joy. I can't explain why, but it is. I love food. I've always loved food, and it makes me happy. - Scott Huggins

The Chef

Scott spent his twenties working in MICHELIN-Starred restaurants in Singapore, Spain and Tokyo, including a stint at Three-MICHELIN-Star restaurant Ryugin under Seiji Yamamoto. He came back to Australia in 2013 with the technique already built in. What he added was restraint. "We work around the ingredients available to us locally," he says. "Each new season brings something different, different ingredients, different tastes, which we use to bring a dish to life." That's where the plate starts telling a South Australian story. Wagyu from Mayura Station on the Limestone Coast, cuts hand-picked and supplied exclusively to Magill Estate. Truffles down from the Adelaide Hills, sometimes shaved over the pasta within a day of being dug. Nothing on the plate has travelled far, and nothing on it needs much done to it. Scott believes that's the whole trick: know how something grew, and where, and the cooking itself gets simpler. "Starting with good produce, we end up with good dishes," he says. "Food is life, it's everything." That's obvious the moment the first course lands. It's just as obvious in the kitchen behind it, where the dishie, the apprentice and the gardener are all still working, long after the last table's gone home.

A day at Penfolds

Your Penfolds Heritage Experience

Start the day where it all began. The Ultimate Penfolds Tour takes you underground into the historic cellars at Magill Estate for a private walk through 180 years of winemaking, capped off with a tasting of rare vintages you won't find anywhere else. Spend the afternoon at your own pace — wander the vines, soak up the city views, let the anticipation build — before the main event: dinner at Magill Estate Restaurant, where seasonal fine dining meets back-vintage Penfolds wines against a backdrop of the very vineyard it all started from.

Where to stay

Wind down at Mount Lofty House Estate, a heritage luxury property tucked into the Adelaide Hills with sweeping valley views and a fine-dining legacy of its own.

Your Two-MICHELIN-Star dinner

Top dishes to order

Trust the wine pairing menu and let the sommeliers guide you toward whatever's sharing the table with a glass of Bin 707 or Grange that night — it's built to be the highlight.
