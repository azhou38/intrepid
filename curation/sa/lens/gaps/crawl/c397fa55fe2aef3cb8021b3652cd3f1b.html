Ngeringa Wines, Adelaide Hills

WELCOME TO ADELAIDE, SOUTH AUSTRALIA

South Australia. Where lakes shimmer pink, gardens grow underground and award winning wines are served inside a Rubik's cube. Here, summer days are spent at beachside bars with your toes in the sand and our world-class chefs are as much scientists as cooks. It also happens to be Australia’s first destination with 77 MICHELIN Guide 2027 recognised restaurants to choose from. In winter, succumb to nature's nurturing embrace in a cosy hidden hamlet or sink into a hot spring in the depths of the outback. In summer, cast away to an island paradise where wildlife reigns supreme or lose yourself down a labyrinth of laneways lined with bars in the bustling city of Adelaide. 

 

 

 

A Guide to Discovering South Australia

South Australia has everything you've dreamt of experiencing in Australia. From our vibrant pink lakes, pristine clear-water beaches to our world-class wineries and restaurants. Start your trip in Adelaide - the heart of South Australia where you will find high-quality dining and lively festivals all year round. Then road trip to the regions of South Australia, and explore our diverse wildlife, jaw-dropping natural wonders and unique tours and experiences. Here is our local guide to the top things you must see and do in South Australia.
