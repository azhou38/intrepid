What's On In South Australia

Handpicked

FIND OUT WHAT'S ON IN SOUTH AUSTRALIA

South Australia loves a party and our world-class events are our way of showcasing our exceptional food, wine, sport, art and boutique capital. In the space of a few months each year, Adelaide becomes host to some of the best events including Adelaide Fringe, Adelaide Festival, WOMADelaide, Santos Tour Down Under and LIV Golf Adelaide. We're also home to AFL Gather Round, Tasting Australia presented by Journey Beyond, Men's Rugby World Cup 2027, and the winter festival lighting up the city, Illuminate Adelaide. The party doesn’t stop in Adelaide though. Our regions will find any excuse for a celebration, with regional events and festivals showing-off the best of our state's food, wine, fashion, art and design all year round. There is always something on in South Australia, so keep busy this month with our calendar of events.

South Australia's Ultimate Events

From the crack of bats at Adelaide Oval to lights spilling across winter nights, mark the calendar with some of Australia’s most popular festivals. Crowds lean over barriers at the Tour Down Under, friends toast at Norwood’s Food & Wine Festival and families line the streets as the Pageant rolls past. These are the moments that shape the South Australian story. Start planning your next chapter.

Regional Events Calendar

Regional festivals invite you to travel further and step closer. Don’t just buy almonds off the shelf—harvest a handful with the farmer who grew them. Celebrate the humble lamb and all it has done for a tiny town, from the clothes on their backs to the meal they share with you. Sip reds that taste of even redder soils and meet artists painting scenes they call home. Each season brings a new reason to connect—with place, produce and people. This is your invitation to mark the calendar, one regional getaway at a time.

  • Tunarama

    Tunarama, Eyre Peninsula - January

    On Port Lincoln’s foreshore, seafood sizzles, music drifts, and tuna flies—where locals and visitors gather to share stories, flavours and the spirit of the sea.

  • Dupang Festival, Murray River Lakes and Coorong

    Dupang Festival, Murray River Lakes & Coorong - March

    Campfires crackle, dancers move with the wind, stories rise with the smoke. By the Coorong, the Dupang Festival brings culture to life—slow, grounding and unforgettable.

  • Barossa Vintage Festival

    Barossa Vintage Festival - April

    Barossa’s wine heritage flows through cellar doors and village streets—where laughter lingers, stories unfold and creativity fills the air in a celebration of community and craft.

  • SALT Festival

    SALT Festival, Eyre Peninsula - April

    Salt Festival celebrates creativity, connection and possibility by the sea — where local stories and flavours meet art, lighting up South Australia's stunning coastal region.

  • Clare Valley Gourmet Festival

    Clare Valley Gourmet Week - May

    Rolling vineyards, soulful wines and long lunches invite you to slow down, savour local flavours and celebrate Clare Valley’s rich food and wine culture together.

  • Gutsy

    Gutsy, Kangaroo Island - June

    Bold local producers, fireside feasts, fine wine and immersive winter experiences come together across Kangaroo Island’s stunning venues during this uniquely spirited winter festival.

  • Coober Peddy Opal Festival

    Coober Peddy Opal Festival, Flinders Ranges & Outback - June

    Opal heritage shines bright in Coober Pedy’s biggest celebration—where underground culture, desert charm and heartfelt community moments create unforgettable simple pleasures.

  • Winter Whale Fest

    Winter Whale Fest, Fleurieu Peninsula - June

    Each winter, coastal communities come alive with whales, art, science, and story—this month-long festival blends marine wonder with culture, creativity and shared discovery in Victor Harbor.

  • Willunga Almond Blossom Festival

    Willunga Almond Blossom Festival, Fleurieu Peninsula - July

    Blossoms bloom, almonds crack and community spirit thrives—this heartfelt festival celebrates Willunga’s rich almond-growing heritage through food, music and joyful moments.

  • Winter reds

    Winter Reds, Adelaide Hills - July

    Crackling fires, soulful reds and hearty feasts—this cosy celebration of cool-climate wine invites you to slow down, savour the winter season and share warm moments together.

  • Fleurieu Food Festival

    Fleurieu Food Festival - August

    A month of flavour, warmth and connection—this festival celebrates local produce, passionate makers and shared moments through long lunches, hands-on feasts and soulful culinary experiences.

  • Grounded YP Art Festival

    Grounded Art Festival, Yorke Peninsula - August

    Grounded is a biennial coastal arts festival where light, fire and storytelling unite—connecting community, culture and creativity across the Yorke Peninsula through shared experiences.

  • The Bend, Murray River Lakes and Coorong

    The Bend, Murray River Lakes & Coorong - September

    Spring ignites The Bend with roaring engines, country tunes and festival energy—where adrenaline, celebration and shared moments create a vibrant escape in South Australia’s heartland.

  • Clare valley festival of the lamb

    Clare Valley Festival of the Lamb - September

    From paddock to plate, this springtime celebration blends lamb, wine, wool and community—where shared meals, craft and culture honour the region’s rich pastoral traditions.

  • Nature Festival, South Australia

    Nature Festival of South Australia - September

    Rooted in Adelaide with events statewide, this festival invites all to reconnect with nature through art, walks, talks and joyful moments that nourish the soul.

  • Oysterfest Ceduna

    Oysterfest Ceduna, Eyre Peninsula - October

    Australia’s oyster capital celebrates community, coastline and culture—where fresh shucks, local pride and laid-back joy create a festival full of flavour and simple pleasures.

  • Grenache and Gourmet festival

    Grenache & Gourmet Festival, Fleurieu Perninsula - October
