Michelin Guide

Your seat at South Australia’s MICHELIN-Starred tables

The world’s most prestigious culinary authority – the MICHELIN Guide – has officially revealed its inaugural Australian selection. The French food bible has awarded a total of 12 MICHELIN Stars to South Australia’s top dining destinations, alongside 16 Bib Gourmand awards for exception quality at accessible prices, and 50 MICHELIN-Selected establishments.

It’s no surprise all of Australia’s MICHELIN-Starred restaurants can be found here, the land of the long, long lunch. Ask a South Australian where to eat and you’ll get three recommendations, directions, and probably the name of the person pouring the wine. Here, plates pass around the table like a map of our diverse landscapes: heirloom tomatoes still warm from rich earth, oysters dripping with cold ocean brine, and saltbush lamb raised on salt-dusted, rolling pastures. In your glass, you’ll taste why Adelaide is one of the official Great Wine Capitals of the World.

There's an open-armed generosity to it all, arriving on a plate or in a glass and rarely in small measures. It’s all shaped by generations of chefs, growers, makers, sommeliers and front-of-house who make a meal feel easy. This recognition is as much theirs, as it is ours.

Discover South Australia’s MICHELIN-Starred and Bib Gourmand selection below. We’ve saved you a seat. All you need to do is show up with an appetite.

South Australia's Two-MICHELIN-Starred restaurant

History made: with Two MICHELIN Stars awarded, Magill Estate Restaurant is one of just roughly 500 restaurants worldwide who can claim this exceptional accolade. To be awarded two stars, a restaurant requires a unanimous consensus among anonymous inspectors that the cuisine is ‘worth a detour’.

One-MICHELIN-Starred restaurants

BIB GOURMAND RESTAURANTS

BIB GOURMAND RESTAURANTS

Nobody in South Australia needs convincing that a great meal can be a simple one. Bib Gourmand, named after Bibendum (the Michelin Man mascot), recognises restaurants that share a simple philosophy: thoughtful cooking, great value and plates that let the ingredients do the talking. Here, that might be handmade pasta, a pie with freshly rolled pastry or a plate full of whatever was harvested fresh that morning. Whatever the cuisine, ask the person serving what's good, then order it.

Meet the restaurants behind the stars

Go beyond the award and discover the stories behind South Australia's most celebrated dining experiences. Meet the chefs, explore their craft, watch their story unfold and uncover what awaits beyond the restaurant door, from local experiences to nearby itineraries worth savouring.

Meet the restaurants behind the Stars

A MICHELIN Star is only the start. Get to know the chefs, growers and makers behind South Australia’s most celebrated tables, and the stories that put each dish on the plate. Then make a day of it, with local experiences and itineraries that keep the long lunch going well.

  • Magill Estate Restaurant

    Magill Estate Restaurant cooks around the cellar

    Scott Huggins
    Magill Estate Restaurant (MER)

    MICHELIN Stars, South Australia 2027

    Experience 180 years of winemaking and an Executive Chef who won’t take the credit alone. This is Magill Estate Restaurant, where local produce and old-vintage wines shape every plate.
  • Arkhé

    Everything at Arkhé meets open flame before it meets you

    Jake Kellie
    Arkhé

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    Everything here meets fire first. Jake Kellie’s wood oven turns local wagyu and lobster into the food that earned Arkhé one of Australia’s first MICHELIN-Stars.

     

  • Restaurant Aptos

    Restaurant Aptos brings native Australia to the table

    Justin James
    Restaurant Aptos

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    Inside an 1800s church in the Adelaide Hills, 14 guests at a time eat their way across 16 courses of native Australia. Come curious and expect to get involved.

     

  • Restaurant Botanic

    Eat what the garden gives up at Restaurant Botanic

    Jamie Musgraves
    Restaurant Botanic

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    The Adelaide Botanic Garden frames the view, then it lands on your plate. Executive Chef Jamie Musgraves’ 15-course menu changes with every season, so no two visits are quite the same.

     

  • Esmay

    Esmay is a love letter to South Australian producers

    Alanna Sapwell
    Esmay

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    Nostalgia on a fork, served inside a 140-year-old former pub. Chef Alanna Sapwell-Stone’s love letter to South Australian growers is part grandmother’s kitchen, part world-class pass.

  • LVN Restaurant

    Dinner at LVN Restaurant is like dining in a painting

    Jacob Davey
    LVN

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    At the end of a winding private drive, a glasshouse restaurant treats the Adelaide Hills like a canvas. The menu starts in the garden and is built backwards from the wine.

  • Hentley Farm Restaurant

    Lunch starts outside the window at Hentley Farm Restaurant

    Kyle Johns
    Hentley Farm Restaurant

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    Lunch starts outside the window. Chef Kyle Johns builds the menu from the garden, the creek flats and growers down the road, with the winemaker working alongside.

     

  • Maxwell Restaurant

    At Maxwell Restaurant the vineyard sets the menu

    Matt Rogers and Fabian Lehmann
    Maxwell Restaurant

    MICHELIN Star, South Australia 2027

    Tucked into a limestone hill in McLaren Vale, this family-owned restaurant builds a multi-course tasting menu around South Australian produce and the family’s own wines.
