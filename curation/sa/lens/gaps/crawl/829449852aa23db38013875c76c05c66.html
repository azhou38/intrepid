The Cockle Train, Fleurieu Peninsula

Experience South Australia on a Budget

Holidays should feel rich in the ways that matter, like a bakery bag rustling in the passenger seat or a sunset you would have paid good money for, if anyone had thought to charge. This page is here for that kind of planning: the clever little wins that help you spend less, without shaving the pleasure off the trip.

Travel solo, bring someone you love or pile into the car with friends and take a road through South Australia’s 12 regions. You might wake in the outback or keep Adelaide as your base, with the beach one way and the Hills the other.

Along the way, you’ll find affordable places to stay, current deals, free things to do and enough low-cost discoveries to make ‘budget’ feel like just a very satisfying bit of holiday strategy. 

 

Holiday Deals

Your guide to affordable South Australian holidays

Build the day around what South Australia gives away freely. A walking trail can take care of the morning before the beach takes over, with a low-cost stay waiting nearby when the day is done. The guides below bring together budget-friendly ways to move through South Australia, from national parks that make for adventure filled days, to campgrounds and cabins that are big on views and small on cost. Keep an eye on our festival and events calendar too, as even ticketed events can spill over with free shows and family-friendly extras.

Free things to do in Adelaide

Adelaide is home to an abundance of simple pleasures that fill your cup, without needing to spill lots of cash. Free attractions can be found right across the city, behind gallery doors, along the river and under the old shade of the parklands. Ride the tram to the coast when the weather is behaving and spend the afternoon dining on local fish and chips as you watch the sunset over the ocean. Just beyond the city centre, conservation parks set the scene for a morning hike. Don’t forget to look up as you go, you may spot a wild koala tucked into the gums. Browse free attractions and events in Adelaide and plan your short stay around what is already happening.

Budget Friendly accommodation in South Australia

Saving on the stay leaves more room for the bits you came for. Choose to instead spend on the long lunch, an extra night, or a hire car that takes you a little further. Across South Australia, you’ll find comfortable budget accommodation, from easy motels to cabins.
If you want the cheapest sleep, camping is hard to beat. Free and low-cost sites are available across the state, though facilities and rules change from place to place. Check before you settle in, then browse top spots below for better value. Reminder: camping outside of designated sites is illegal.

Holidays by the road

A road trip has a way of stripping travel back to the simple things. It’s the joy of watching the landscape change through the window, spotting an echidna ambling beside the track or pulling up for a pub counter lunch in a small town.

Just keep a steady eye on the trail. Read the signs before turning down unsealed roads - which are better suited to a 4WD - and plan your stops around daylight, as wildlife loves to wander at dawn and dusk.

If your dates are flexible, you can also look into vehicle relocations. Platforms like CoSeats and Transfercar offer one-way campervan rentals from $1 a day, making it a low-cost way to explore further.

Popular Road trips & itinerary ideas

Start close, then stretch the kilometres when the budget allows. These itineraries keep the good stuff within reach, with fuel doing most of the heavy lifting.

  • Melbourne to Adelaide drive

    Drive from Melbourne to Adelaide

    The direct route runs 728km, but it does not need to feel like transit. Take the scenic way and let bakeries, beaches and old stone towns interrupt you.

  • Flinders Ranges itinerary

    Flinders Ranges itinerary

    The further north you go, the more the noise drops away. More than 600km from Adelaide, the road opens into ranges and quiet camp nights.

  • The Ultimate Clare Valley itinerary

    The Ultimate Clare Valley itinerary

    Around 140km from Adelaide, this short stay keeps fuel low and days full: cycle the Riesling Trail, visit heritage towns and cellar doors.

  • Seafood Frontier

    Seafood Frontier

    This 955km route follows the appetite as much as the coast. Save with simple stays, then spend where the oysters are pulled from the water.

  • The ultimate guide to exploring outback South Australia

    The ultimate guide to exploring outback South Australia

    This guide heads below the surface, in every sense. In Coober Pedy, dugout stays and opal fever turn a simple holiday into something wonderfully offbeat.

  • Explorers Way

    Explorers Way

    The 1,142km South Australian stretch of the iconic pilgrimage from Adelaide to Darwin might ask more of the fuel tank, but pays back with outback towns, campgrounds and a blanket of stars.

  • Limestone Coast itinerary

    Limestone Coast itinerary

    About 340km to Robe, this six-day itinerary drops into Naracoorte’s fossil caves before surfacing for Robe’s coast and Coonawarra cabernet.

  • Southern Ocean Drive

    Southern Ocean Drive

    A 940km drive with plenty to pull over for. Spend on fuel, then let sinkholes, surf towns and big Southern Ocean views do the rest.

  • Epicurean Way

    Epicurean Way

    At 223km, this delicious drive loops through four premier food regions. Your main job here is tracking down fresh bakery treats and local farm gates.

  • Yorke Peninsula itinerary

    Yorke Peninsula itinerary

    Just over an hour from Adelaide, Yorke Peninsula gives you 700km of coastline to work with. Beach walks and jetty stops do plenty for free.

  • Coastal Way

    Coastal Way

    This 460km Yorke Peninsula route keeps the sea close, with lighthouses, surf beaches, shipwrecks and low-cost campsites near national parks.
