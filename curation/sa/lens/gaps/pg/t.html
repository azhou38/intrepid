Tanunda

Rogasch Cottage, Barossa

Explore Tanunda: The heart of the Barossa

The red heart of the Barossa, Tanunda sits in prime position surrounded by the region’s best wineries. Deriving its name from the Aboriginal word for watering hole, you’ll certainly find more than a few good spots to quench your thirst around this historic town. Sample wine straight from the barrel at Château Tanunda, go back to (wine) school at Greenock Estates or sip bold reds in a heritage stone barn at Rockford Wines. Wander down leafy streets dotted with heritage buildings, stop for lunch at Vino Lokal, or dig into dinner at Harvest Kitchen. Down the road, discover Seppeltsfield; home to Seppeltsfield Winery, Hentley Farm Restaurant and Seppeltsfield Road Distillers.
