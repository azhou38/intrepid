DISCOVER THE ARTISAN AUSTRALIA

Home of Plenty, Fleurieu Peninsula

Discover the best of Australia, in South Australia

Start your journey by flying direct to the lively city of Adelaide with United Airlines. Our boutique capital is home to festivals, bars and MICHELIN Guide award-winning restaurants. If you're after world-class wines, visit our regions and meet the people who make them - from Barossa, Coonawarra, Adelaide Hills and McLaren Vale, South Australia has the best wines you'll find in Australia. For an outback adventure, head to the Flinders Ranges & Outback and book an underground hotel, or a glamping tent with the view of Ikara Flinders Ranges National Park. Want to escape to an island full of Australian wildlife and our iconic Aussie Kangaroos? Head to Kangaroo Island to see a range of unique wildlife on the secluded coastal island.

South Australian NATURAL WONDERS AND WILDLIFE

More than just cuddly koalas, South Australia is home to abundant wildlife. From friendly sea lions, giant cuttlefish, racing emus and wild kangaroos. Get up close with South Australia’s wildlife in the wild. That’s right - no fences, no glass, just you meeting your latest Aussie mate in their natural habitat. Or, if you want to step back and take it all in, check out our incredible South Australian walks that showcase the very best of our pristine environment.

THE OUTBACK ON ADELAIDE'S DOORSTEP

Less than five hours drive from the vibrant city of Adelaide and you’ll be in the outback of South Australia. Here you can discover the rugged beauty of the Flinders Ranges, dig for treasure and sleep underground the mining town of Coober Pedy, or star gaze on the peaks of Arkaroola. One thing’s for sure, once you visit the outback, it will never leave you.
