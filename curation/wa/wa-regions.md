# Western Australia: coverage regions (run curation/wa)

The whole state is split into seven regions, following Tourism WA's five (Experience Perth, Australia's South West,
Australia's Golden Outback, Australia's Coral Coast, Australia's North West) with the North West split into the Pilbara
and the Kimberley, and the South West into the South West and the Great Southern. Each region pass covers ALL of its
land: towns, parks, coast, islands, outback and the roads between them. A place on a boundary belongs to the region it
lies in by the lines below; if unsure, include it and say so in its `notes` (the proposer resolves duplicates).

- **wa-perth — Perth & Surrounds.** Greater Perth (all metro councils, Fremantle, Cottesloe, Kings Park, Swan River),
  Rottnest Island, Swan Valley, the Perth Hills (Kalamunda, Mundaring, John Forrest NP, Serpentine), Rockingham and
  Penguin Island, the Peel (Mandurah, Pinjarra, Dwellingup, Waroona, Yalgorup NP), and the Avon Valley (York, Northam,
  Toodyay, Beverley) plus New Norcia and Gingin/Yanchep.
- **wa-south-west — South West.** The South West administrative region: Harvey, Australind, Bunbury, Collie, Donnybrook,
  Busselton, Dunsborough, Cape Naturaliste, Yallingup, Margaret River and its wine region, Leeuwin-Naturaliste NP, Augusta,
  Cape Leeuwin, Nannup, Balingup, Bridgetown, Boyup Brook, Manjimup, Pemberton, Northcliffe and the Southern Forests,
  D'Entrecasteaux NP west of Walpole.
- **wa-great-southern — Great Southern.** The Great Southern administrative region: Walpole and Walpole-Nornalup NP (Valley
  of the Giants), Denmark, Albany and Torndirrup NP, Two Peoples Bay, Mount Barker, Porongurup NP, Stirling Range NP,
  Kojonup, Katanning, Gnowangerup, Bremer Bay, and the WHOLE of Fitzgerald River NP.
- **wa-golden-outback — Golden Outback & Wheatbelt.** The Goldfields-Esperance region (Kalgoorlie-Boulder, Coolgardie,
  Kambalda, Norseman, Esperance, Cape Le Grand NP, Cape Arid NP, Lucky Bay, Ravensthorpe and Hopetoun (but not Fitzgerald
  River NP), Leonora, Gwalia, Laverton, Menzies and Lake Ballard, the Nullarbor and Eucla, the Great Victoria Desert), the
  Wheatbelt except the Avon Valley and the coastal strip (Hyden and Wave Rock, Merredin, Kulin, Corrigin, Narrogin,
  Wagin, Dryandra Woodland, Southern Cross), and the Murchison inland (Mount Magnet, Cue, Meekatharra, Sandstone, Wiluna,
  Walga Rock).
- **wa-coral-coast — Coral Coast & Mid West.** The coast and its hinterland from Lancelin and the Turquoise Coast north
  to Exmouth: Lancelin, Cervantes and Nambung NP (the Pinnacles), Jurien Bay, Lesueur NP, Green Head, Dongara,
  Geraldton, the Houtman Abrolhos, Northampton, Horrocks, Kalbarri NP, Shark Bay (Monkey Mia, Denham, Francois Peron NP,
  Shell Beach, Hamelin Pool), Carnarvon, Quobba, Coral Bay, Ningaloo Reef, Exmouth and Cape Range NP, and the Gascoyne
  inland (Kennedy Range NP, Mount Augustus NP), plus the Mid West wildflower towns (Mullewa, Morawa, Three Springs,
  Coalseam Conservation Park).
- **wa-pilbara — Pilbara.** Onslow, Karratha, Dampier, Murujuga NP (Burrup), Roebourne, Cossack, Point Samson,
  Millstream-Chichester NP, Karijini NP, Tom Price, Paraburdoo, Newman, Port Hedland, Marble Bar, Rudall River /
  Karlamilyi NP, the Dampier Archipelago, the Montebello Islands.
- **wa-kimberley — Kimberley.** Broome (Cable Beach, Gantheaume Point, Roebuck Bay), Eighty Mile Beach, the Dampier
  Peninsula (Cape Leveque, Beagle Bay, Lombadina), Derby, the Buccaneer Archipelago and Horizontal Falls, the Gibb River
  Road (Windjana Gorge, Tunnel Creek, Bell Gorge, Manning Gorge, Mount Elizabeth, Home Valley, El Questro), Fitzroy
  Crossing and Danggu Geikie Gorge, Halls Creek, Wolfe Creek Crater, Purnululu NP (Bungle Bungles), Kununurra, Lake
  Argyle, Mirima NP, Wyndham and the Five Rivers Lookout, the Mitchell Plateau and Mitchell Falls, the Kimberley coast
  (King George Falls, Montgomery Reef), and the Tanami edge.

The lists name examples, not limits: enumerate from sources across the whole of each region.
Country: Australia (AU). Timezone: Australia/Perth. Currency: AUD. Managing agency for parks: Parks and Wildlife Service
(DBCA), exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au. Tourism board: westernaustralia.com (Tourism WA) and the five regional
organisations named above. Wikivoyage: "Western Australia" and its region and town pages.
