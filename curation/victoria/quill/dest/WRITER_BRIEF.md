# Destination copy writer brief (Victoria, Pipeline C step 2)
Role: .claude/agents/copy-writer.md. Rules: WRITING.md "Writing a destination" and "Voice" (read both first).
Input: your wr-in-N.json (destination id, name, rank, category, spot names, research_file). Read each destination's research record (in the research_file, match by id; the file may be a list or {destinations:[...]}) and write ONLY from its sourced facts.
Output: curation/victoria/quill/dest/wr-out-N.json, a list; per destination one object:
{ id, icon (single emoji), tagline (ONE sentence, fills exactly two lines of the sheet header, about 60-85 characters), description (3-4 sentences, concrete: what it is, why people go, one distinctive fact), whyVisit (2 for rank 5, 3 for rank 3-4, 4 for rank 1-2; each 2-8 words, starts with an action verb, GENERAL experiences, not spot names, except an iconic attraction core to the destination), highlights (one 2-3 word phrase per reason), goodToKnow (3 tips for rank 1-3, 2 for rank 4-5: {icon, title 2-5 words, detail <=160 chars, aim ~120}), bestMonths ([1-12], southern-hemisphere), bestTimeBlurb (exactly one {months} placeholder, 60-110 chars with months filled in, no semicolons; says when to go and why), seasonalTags (only a real recurring pattern; omit otherwise), unverified:[fields whose basis is the researcher's own inference, e.g. bestMonths with no source], notes }
Rules: facts from the research record only; never name a source in the copy; no marketing filler or unbackable superlatives; no transient promises (eruptions, one-off events, temporary exhibitions); no repetition between description, reasons, tips and tagline; no semicolons in blurb. If the research record lacks material for a field, write what it supports and list the gap in `unverified`; never invent. A rank-5 destination gets 2 reasons and 2 tips even if the research has more.
Final message at most 5 lines: file written, counts, gaps.
