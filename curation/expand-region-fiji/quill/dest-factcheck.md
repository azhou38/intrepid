# Fiji destination fact-check (9 entries)

Sources read: Wikivoyage and Wikipedia pages for each destination and key spots, plus the spot research notes. Tagline and blurb fit checks all pass.

## Changes (all destinations)
- bestTimeBlurb, all nine: months kept at May-Oct (Wikivoyage Fiji advises the southern winter; cyclones Nov-Apr per Wikivoyage and Wikipedia). Claim "dry season" / "wet" replaced by "cooler months" and "Cyclones can occur Nov to Apr." Suva: Wikipedia says no true dry season (July driest), so blurb says "driest stretch... Rain falls all year."

## Coral Coast
- Description: removed "boats to Beqa Island" (unsourced), "18th-century" fort (century not confirmed; now "built by a Tongan chief"). Beqa Island 10 km offshore from spot notes.
- whyVisit 3: "Raft a rainforest gorge" -> "Raft a waterfall-lined gorge" (rainforest unsourced).
- Tips: Biausevu "best after wet season" and "20-minute, 20 m tiers" unsupported (source: about 30 minutes, nine stream crossings, fee paid in village); Pacific Harbour tip limited to the shark dive; rafting "three to six hours, reached from Navua" -> about eight hours (listing), road access dropped.

## Mamanuca Islands

## Suva

## Vanua Levu

Waya Lailai "rocky volcanic peak" and "three-hour walk to Naboro" unsourced (research dropped it) -> sunrise climb, guides optional, spit walk to Waya.
