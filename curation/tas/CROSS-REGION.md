# Tasmania: shared placement rules for the six region proposers

Six regions are proposed in parallel, so places that appear in two regions have ONE owner; the other region drops it from placement:
- Bay of Fires, Binalong Bay, Eddystone Point Lighthouse, St Helens, Mount William National Park, wukalina/Mount William walk, Beaconsfield Mine: owner = tas-launceston-northeast. tas-tasman-east-coast drops them (and Stumpys Bay, Ansons Bay, Scamander if reviewed).
- Cradle Mountain-Lake St Clair National Park: owner = tas-northwest-cradle (destination "Cradle Mountain" if it clears the bar). tas-west-coast-southwest keeps Lake St Clair, Echo Point, Mount Ossa and the Overland Track as spots of that destination or standalone, but does not propose the park again.
- Anything in Southwest National Park / Cockle Creek / South Coast Track appears in both hobart-southeast and west-coast-southwest: owner = tas-west-coast-southwest for Southwest NP, Gordon Dam, Lake Pedder, South Coast Track; hobart-southeast keeps Cockle Creek, Recherche Bay is rejected.

Destination rules (COVERAGE.md): a destination needs at least four spots or rank 1-2 flagship status; a name travellers use; a town beside its own attractions is not a spot; a place filed under a bigger city is its spot; a national park is never a spot of another park. Likely destination candidates to test across the whole island (propose only if they clear the bar, each with `name_evidence`): Hobart, Launceston, Cradle Mountain, Freycinet, Tasman Peninsula (Port Arthur), Bruny Island, Maria Island, Bay of Fires/St Helens, Mount Field, Strahan, Southwest, Stanley, Flinders Island, King Island. Existing standalone/destination spots in the app are all non-Tasmanian (false name matches were cleared); there are no Tasmanian entries in the app yet.

Data fixes the proposers must apply: Lake St Clair = Tasmanian lake near Cynthia Bay (about -42.12, 146.18; wiki "Lake St Clair (Tasmania)"; its views/coordinates in candidates.json belong to Michigan). Lewisham, Pool of Bethesda and Oyster Cove style name-collision candidates: views and coordinates in candidates.json may belong to another place; take coordinates only from `python3 scripts/curation/resolve-pin.py`. Greens Beach (NW) is ambiguous: do not place until resolved, put it in the review list. Mersey Bluff Lighthouse: name the spot Mersey Bluff if the draw is the bluff. wukalina Walk: name the spot for the summit walk, not the operator tour. Time zone for all Tasmania entries: Australia/Hobart. Never name a source in copy. Entries are drafts: facts and pins researched later, never placeholders.
