Group folders: curation/tas/lens/<group>/task.json. Slot kinds: `spot:<id>` (the spot's own photo, 960 px+), `dest:<id>:header` (a destination header, at least 1500 px wide, near-square crop friendly, 3:2/4:3/portrait, a spectacular wide scene of the place), `dest:<id>:why1..why4` (one photo per whyVisit reason of that destination, 1280 px+; read the reason text in src/data/destinations.ts and show THAT thing). status `search` = no photo (placeholder), `confirm` = judge the current photo. Rules that matter most here: (1) four checks incl. right place: Tasmanian places share names with others (Lake St Clair, Cloudy Bay, Swansea, Richmond, Devonport NZ/Auckland, Bruny, Sheffield UK, Fossil Bluff, Hobart Oregon...): search with "Tasmania" and confirm on the file page by categories/geotag; (2) NO TWO SPOTS SHARE A PHOTO OR SUBJECT: compare with spots within ~10 km (Richmond/Richmond Bridge/Richmond Gaol; Cataract Gorge and Duck Reach; Port Arthur, Isle of the Dead etc.; the Cradle Mountain family: Dove Lake, summit walk, Marion's Lookout, Waldheim); a destination header/why photo MAY reuse one of its own spots' photos; (3) current colour photos from 2000+, no maps/drawings/aerials for headers; crop check with python3 scripts/curation/hero-crop.py; brightness floor noted. Commons first (geosearch near the coordinates + Tasmania categories); if Commons has no passing photo, go to the non-Commons tiers per IMAGES.md (Openverse via scripts/openverse-search.py, then official sources, and the in-app browser is NOT available to you: list those slots as needs-a-browser). Fetch pages with `npx tsx scripts/curation/fetch.ts`; pace Wikimedia at about 1 request/second and back off 30/60/120 s on 429.
Output per group: candidates.json ({slot:[{file|url,page,width,height,licence,why}]}) and judge-1.json ({slot:{candidates:[…], choice:'<file>'|'keep-current'|'none: <why>', hero_crop:'ok'|{x,y,zoom}|'fail: …'}}). Final message: slots where you chose a NEW file (these go to a second judge), then one line per other slot, and the slots that need a browser.
