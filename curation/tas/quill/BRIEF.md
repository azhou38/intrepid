# Tasmania spot research brief (one group file: curation/tas/quill/in-<G>.json; write curation/tas/quill/research-<G>.json)

Role: .claude/agents/spot-researcher.md. Rules: WRITING.md "Writing a spot" and "Voice". For EVERY spot in your input file write one entry:
{ id, name, coordinates:{lat,lng}, coordinates_source (source + feature type), pin_confidence (HIGH|MEDIUM|LOW|NONE), moved_m (distance from the input point), hours (real range or "Open 24 hours" or a short qualifier; never "varies"), closedDays ([0-6] or null), visitHoursMin, visitHoursMax (range, quarter-hour steps; quick stops start at 0), free (true) OR costMin/costMax + currency "AUD", ticketUrl (verified official site, or null with reason), blocked_url (URL if BLOCKED, else null), bio (final one sentence, at least 10 words, about 110 characters or fewer so it fits three card lines; plain, specific, no marketing, no source names, no "stunning/must-see"), unverified:[what you could not verify], sources:[urls] }.
Pins: from `python3 scripts/curation/resolve-pin.py --name .. --category .. --lat .. --lon .. [--wiki ..] --json` (HIGH or MEDIUM only); for a trail/track/walk pin the MIDPOINT of the route (resolve-pin reports the OSM way midpoint; for a multi-day track the centre of its whole length); a summit/lookout is pinned on the feature; never from memory, never rounded to 3 decimals or fewer, never a town centre. Overpass (overpass-api.de) is down: use the mirror https://overpass.kumi.systems/api/interpreter (give a User-Agent, pace 1 request/second, back off on 429), Wikipedia/Commons geotags, and the managing agency's map (Parks and Wildlife Service Tasmania, parks.tas.gov.au). On LOW, take the agency's trailhead/car park/entrance point and say so; if still unsure set pin_confidence NONE rather than guess. Do not use candidates.json coordinates that belong to another place with the same name (Lake St Clair/Michigan, Cloudy Bay/NZ, Lewisham/London, Swansea/Wales, North Cave/England, Fossil Bluff/Antarctica).
ticketUrl: the one official site (operator, or the managing agency page for a park/track; Parks Tasmania parks.tas.gov.au for public land). Load it and check the heading names the place. A 403/captcha = BLOCKED. Never an aggregator. Hours/prices from the operator's own page. Entry fees: Tasmanian parks charge a Parks Pass per vehicle: record the adult/vehicle day price as costMin/costMax, not free. Check closures that affect visitors now (today is 2026-10-20), and mention in unverified.
Fetch with `npx tsx scripts/curation/fetch.ts <url>` (shared cache). Names follow WRITING.md (own name only, title case). Keep the input name unless it is wrong (Mersey Bluff, Little Penguin Viewing Area already renamed). Write ONLY your research file. Final message: counts of HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/NONE pins, spots with no ticketUrl, BLOCKED urls, anything that looks wrong or should be dropped (a place that is closed, private, or not a visitor stop).
