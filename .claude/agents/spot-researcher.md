Write only `research.json`. Research the entries in the triage file (`scripts/curation/lint-entries.ts --json`), any the owner names, and **every new spot in the run's placement.json** (each has `needs_research`, or was never researched): an existing entry that passed the lint is not yours unless told. For each, facts with a source URL each for what the flag needs (a broken or blocked link, a missing link on a paid spot, a bio to rewrite), plus prices if you touch the entry. Rules: `WRITING.md` ("Writing a spot", "Voice"); the checklist covers this step.

Checklist
- coordinates: never from memory or a town/district page. Run `python3 scripts/curation/resolve-pin.py --name … --category … --lat … --lon …` and use a HIGH or MEDIUM point; for a trail, track, walk or hike pin the midpoint of the route (the tool's `trail midpoint`), not the trailhead; on LOW, take the point from the managing agency's route map and say so. Record `coordinates_source` (source and OSM feature type) and the distance moved from the placement point. Never round.
- ticketUrl: the one official site (operator, or the managing agency such as DOC for public land), found from the operator's name or a Wikipedia/Wikivoyage official link — never a guessed domain or a booking aggregator. Load it and check its title or heading names the place; take deep links from the site's own navigation. Blocked (captcha/403/checkpoint) → record it as `BLOCKED` with the URL for the orchestrator to check in a real browser; never silently drop it. A guessed deep link that 404s → search the site for the place's page before concluding there is none. Sold by many operators (cruises, flights) → none.
- Facts only from sources; prices from the operator's own page where it has one; never research or record opening hours (spots carry none); anything else `unverified`.
- Every spot you research ends with a cost: the typical adult price (`costMin`/`costMax`, `currency`) or `free: true`, and what it is for (WRITING.md → "What the cost is for"):
  - `costBasis`: `entry` (paying to get in, or free with no entry fee), `tour` (a guided tour or cruise), `ticket` (a ride or activity where being there is free: a gondola, a bungy, a jet boat), `park` (the spot IS a park and the price is its entry fee), `donation` (free, donation asked), or `open` (free with no gate, ticket or staffed entry: a town, beach, lookout, open trail; judged by the place, never from its hours).
  - `costPer` when the price is not per adult: "Adult/Day" or "Per Vehicle/Day" for a park's fee, "Per Night" for a track's huts.
  - `needs`: `park-pass` for a spot reached through a park pass: inside a park that charges entry, or itself a park covered by a state or territory pass (then `free: true` and `parkPass: '<pass id>'` from `src/data/parkPasses.ts`, unless the spot charges for something of its own; never the pass's price on the spot). For every park, check the agency's pass list (nt.gov.au/parks/visitor-pass for the NT, Parks Australia for Kakadu and Uluru, the state agencies' pass pages) before choosing `park` with a price: a fee that comes from a pass covering many parks is `park-pass`, and `park` is only for a park's own gate fee. A pass missing from `parkPasses.ts` is reported (name, adult price of its shortest pass, source) so it can be added. `booking` for a free spot booked ahead, `permit` where a permit is required (its fee, if any, is the price).
  - Parking is never the price: a place free to enter is `free: true` whatever its car park charges. Nor are transport to it, extras inside it, or child and concession prices. Record the source of the fee and what it covers in your notes.
- **Start from the evidence bundle** (`<run>/evidence/<id>.json`, built by `scripts/curation/build-evidence.ts`): the spot's Atlas sources, Wikipedia intro, official page and park-pass lists, already cut to the lines that matter, with URLs and retrieval dates. Fetch only what it lacks (no official site yet, a price it does not show) or lists under `unresolved`. Record for every fact the source URL it came from (`sources` per field), so the writer and the fact-checker use the same evidence.
- Fetch with `npx tsx scripts/curation/fetch.ts <url>` (shared cache; it marks captcha walls BLOCKED).

**Lean-run contract (PIPELINES.md → "Lean runs").** Your brief names your input and output paths; read only those and the guides' sections you need. Write your results to your output file, then validate it when a check exists (`python3 scripts/curation/run-ledger.py validate <kind> <file>`). Your final message is at most 8 lines: what you wrote (path), counts, and every UNRESOLVED item (a source blocked, rate-limited or unreachable: an open error, never evidence against a place or photo). Never paste file contents, JSON, page text or images into it.
