You are the single merge step that edits `src/data/*`. Apply `final.json` and the photos the judges agreed (a new file only where both judges chose it; a first-judge keep-current needs nothing). If no honest photo exists for a reason, don't ship a mismatched photo; still apply the spot or destination, and list the slot in the run's final report under "Photos still missing" so the owner can supply one. A missing photo never holds a spot or destination back. Before applying a spot, it must have researched hours, a cost (`free: true` or a price) and a `ticketUrl` decision (a verified link, or a stated reason for none); hold any that don't and list them. Then run `npx tsx scripts/curation/lint-entries.ts --region <id> --offline` (no `no-ticket` line may remain for a spot you wrote), `npx tsc --noEmit -p .`, `npx tsx scripts/check-destination-headers.ts`, `npx tsx scripts/audit-spot-coordinates.ts --dest <id>`, `python3 scripts/curation/check-pin-elevation.py --ids <the hill, peak, lookout and summit spots you wrote>` (a flagged pin is moved onto the feature), `npx tsx scripts/photo-prefilter.ts`, `npx tsx scripts/build-image-manifest.ts`, `python3 scripts/curation/bio-fit.py --ids <the spots you wrote>`, `python3 scripts/curation/photo-brightness.py --keys <the photo keys you changed>`, and fix what they report (a photo below the brightness floor is brightened with `brighten-apply.py`, IMAGES.md → "Brightness", unless it is dark on purpose) (a bio past the carousel card's three lines is trimmed to fit: cut words, not the facts that matter). Write each accepted photo's `hero_crop` focus (`{x, y, zoom}`, any of the three, from the judges' output; `zoom` fixes a landscape photo whose subject sits low or high under a lot of sky or ground) into `PHOTO_SUBJECT` in `src/data/imageOverrides.ts`, keyed by the spot's photo key (its `wiki` title, else its name). Record photo outcomes with `npx tsx scripts/curation/record-verdicts.ts --task … --judge1 … [--judge2 …]` so the next run skips them. One commit per destination.

End your report with the lists required by `PIPELINES.md` → "The run's final report": every spot or destination you skipped or held (with the reason, including a name collision or a missing price), every weak photo or approximate value you accepted, every slot still needing the owner, and what you did not verify. Nothing silent.
