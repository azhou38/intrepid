Take `drafts.json` minus whatever `facts.json` marks unsupported. Write only `final.json`: one version per changed field. Checklist (WRITING.md "Voice"): plain and specific, no filler or unbackable superlatives, no repetition within an entry or with neighbouring destinations in the same country; one sentence for taglines and spot bios, and every tagline fills exactly two lines of the destination header (about 60–85 characters: lengthen a one-liner with a concrete detail, cut a three-liner; `python3 scripts/curation/tagline-fit.py --text "…"` measures it), and every spot bio fits the carousel card's three lines: 110 characters or fewer is safe, cut words rather than key facts (the reconciler measures it exactly); two to four reasons by popularity (4 for rank 1–2, 3 for rank 3–4, 2 for rank 5; 2–8 words each; each reason names a kind of experience, not one of the destination's spots (Kaikōura: "Watch sperm whales year-round", "See mountains meet the sea"), except an attraction that is core or iconic to the destination, which is named (the Opera House for Sydney): rewrite a reason that is just a spot's name dressed up, such as "See the drop at Gibraltar Falls" → "See waterfalls tumble from the ranges"), one highlight per reason, two or three tips (3 for rank 1–3, 2 for rank 4–5) with each detail 160 characters or fewer (cut to ~120 where you can); one `{months}`, and every bestTimeBlurb fills two or three lines of the When to Visit card (about 60–110 characters with the months in; `python3 scripts/curation/blurb-fit.py --text "…" --months 12,1,2` measures it). Keep names and ids exactly.

- Never name a source in the app text (no "Wikivoyage says", "per the brief", "according to ..."): state the fact plainly; sources stay in notes. (WRITING.md → Voice; lint flags it as `source`.)
