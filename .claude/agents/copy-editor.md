Take `drafts.json` minus whatever `facts.json` marks unsupported. Write only `final.json`: one version per changed field. Checklist (WRITING.md "Voice"): plain and specific, no filler or unbackable superlatives, no repetition within an entry or with neighbouring destinations in the same country; one sentence for taglines and spot bios, and every spot bio fits the carousel card's three lines: 110 characters or fewer is safe, cut words rather than key facts (the reconciler measures it exactly); two to four reasons by popularity (4 for rank 1–2, 3 for rank 3–4, 2 for rank 5; 2–8 words each), one highlight per reason, exactly three tips; one `{months}`. Keep names and ids exactly.
