The script `scripts/curation/gather-photo-candidates.py` has already searched every `search` slot (the Wikipedia lead image, Commons geosearch and name searches), filtered, de-duplicated, downloaded and drawn a numbered contact sheet per slot (`sheets/<slot>.jpg`), and written `candidates.json`. The script has already searched Commons by name and by location. **You work only the gaps your brief names**: slots the script found no candidates for, slots whose candidates the first judge rejected, and the sources listed under `_unresolved` (retry them; for a site that blocks scripts, the in-app browser tier). Never search again a slot the script filled and the judge has not rejected. Your value is in the tiers a script cannot reach: tourism-board and parks-agency pages, operator sites, Commons categories, the browser step. Append your finds to that slot's list in `candidates.json` with the same fields (`file` or `url`/`local`, `page`, `width`, `height`, `licence`, `by`, `source`), and remove an `_unresolved` entry only once you have actually reached that source. Write `candidates.json` as `{ slot: [{ file, page, width, height, licence, why }] }`, up to 6 per slot, best first. Rules: `IMAGES.md`; the checklist below covers searching, so open the guide only for a case it doesn't.

Checklist
- **Always check the place's Wikipedia article first**: its lead image (the default photo for a spot, `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&prop=pageimages&piprop=original|name&redirects=1&titles=<wiki title>`) is the first candidate for the slot unless it is plainly wrong (a map, logo, another place). Put it in `candidates.json` even when keyword searches find better-looking files, so the judges see it; a spot is never left with no photo, or marked NO_PHOTO, without the lead image having been offered. (Adelaide River shipped as a placeholder because its lead image, a lookout view of the town, was never a candidate.)
- Commons first: region-qualified queries ("Clyde, Central Otago", never "Clyde"), the place's Commons category, geosearch near its coordinates (`scripts/commons-candidates.py` writes contact sheets). Prefer Featured/Quality/Valued images.
- Minimum width: header 1500 px (aspect 0.6–2.0, ~3:2), why-visit 1280 px (landscape), spot 960 px. Current colour photographs only — no maps, drawings, aerials for headers, archive/B&W scans, logos, signs, a person or crowd as the main focus (people in the frame are fine when the place is the subject). Not a file already used for a DIFFERENT destination (`src/data/imageOverrides.ts`, `whyVisitPhotos`); one destination's header, why-visit photos and spots may share a file, so when you find a photo better than the current header or spot photo, propose it for that slot even if it is already one of the destination's why-visit photos (and the reverse: a great header or spot photo may be proposed as a why-visit photo of the same destination). The Waitomo glowworm-boat photo is the header, a why-visit photo and the Glowworm Caves spot's photo.
- A why-visit photo must show its reason (read the destination's `whyVisit`), not the place in general.
- With nothing usable on Commons: DOC Creative Commons images, then official photos (IMAGES.md "When Commons has no suitable photo").
- Licences: CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA and CC BY-ND are all usable. The app's crop, zoom and brightening count as display, not an edit. NC licences are not usable. An official photo from a tourism board or parks agency (state or regional tourism sites and their ATDW listing photos, DOC, Parks Australia, state parks services, trail bodies such as Walking SA) is used with the owner's permission: licence "official photo, used with permission". Any other official source (an operator, a council, a business) gets "official photo, permission not confirmed".
- For spot photos prefer a photo whose subject is central or fills the frame: the full-screen hero shows only a near-square middle slice of it (IMAGES.md → "Keeping the subject in the crop"), so a wide photo with the object at one edge needs an off-centre crop the judges then have to fix.
- Don't rank or judge; that's the judges'. On Wikimedia 429, wait 30/60/120 s and say so. Fetch file pages with `npx tsx scripts/curation/fetch.ts <url>` (shared cache).
- When Commons, openly licensed and official sources all fail AND an official, operator or tourism page was blocked to scripts, do not stop: use the in-app browser (IMAGES.md → tier 4: open the site, `fetch()` its sibling pages in-page, read each image's alt text, download the originals with curl). If this run has no browser, put the slot in your report as "needs a browser" with the blocked URLs.
- For a spot photo, do not propose a photo whose subject is another spot's (a park's photo is not its waterfall when the waterfall is its own spot; a town's is not its bridge): find something else about the place, or report none (IMAGES.md → "Spot photos": no two spots share a photo or subject).
- A stand-in is a reject: the photo must show the named place itself (not a nearby view, a similar species, another venue, or a photo whose focus is a generic animal or person with the place barely visible). People and animals are fine when they are not the main focus: visitors in a pool or on a boardwalk, an animal in its enclosure with the park around it. If only stand-ins exist the answer is `none`, and the spot becomes NO_PHOTO (IMAGES.md, "A photo shows the place itself").

**Lean-run contract (PIPELINES.md → "Lean runs").** Your brief names your input and output paths; read only those and the guides' sections you need. Write your results to your output file, then validate it when a check exists (`python3 scripts/curation/run-ledger.py validate <kind> <file>`). Your final message is at most 8 lines: what you wrote (path), counts, and every UNRESOLVED item (a source blocked, rate-limited or unreachable: an open error, never evidence against a place or photo).
