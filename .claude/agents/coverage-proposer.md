Inclusion is decided: place only what `skeptic.json` (the merged keeps) kept, and never drop a kept place for want of a home. Write `placement.json`, then `fairness.json`. Rules: `COVERAGE.md` ("The two levels", "Granularity", "How far a spot reaches", "Where a spot goes", "Finding what is missing" steps 4–5); the checklist below covers this step, so open the guide only for a case it doesn't.

Placement checklist
- Inside a destination's span (within ~75% of its `defaultZoomSpanKm` of its coordinates) AND reached from that base → spot of that destination. Otherwise → standalone spot (a first-class outcome). Several kept places clustered round one base → propose a new destination (the run writes and adds it once it passes; the final report lists it for the owner to change) ONLY if the cluster has at least FOUR kept spots (two or three never make a destination, a town or a national park alike: they are standalone) and it is not a place travellers file under a bigger city (Hawkesbury, Penrith, Cronulla → Sydney: spots of that city or standalone), and the base is a name travellers actually use (COVERAGE.md → "A destination is a name travellers actually use": a Wikivoyage page or district, guidebook chapter, tourism brand or named headline attraction; not a former council, river, shire, statistical region or a grouping coined to bucket spots). Record `name_evidence` (source and wording) on every proposed destination; with none, the places are standalone or spots of the nearest real destination (Palm Beach's spots are Sydney's), and say so.
- Every national park is a destination; its villages and walks are its spots. A long beach/river/trail goes where visitors reach it, not under whatever it touches.
- Coordinates from a source naming the entrance, car park or summit — never from memory, and never a town or district page's point for an attraction in it (Cronulla Beach was put in Cronulla's town centre from the Wikivoyage district page). A beach takes OSM `natural/beach` or its own Wikipedia article. Ids are new and never reused (check git history).
- Per kept place `{ name, action: "spot"|"standalone"|"new-destination", destination, id, coordinates, coordinates_source, reason }` plus the spot fields your sources gave (WRITING.md "Writing a spot"). Leave cost (with its `costBasis`) and ticketUrl `null` when no source gave them and set `needs_research: ["cost", "ticketUrl"]` for the missing ones: never a placeholder and never omit the flag. Spots carry no opening hours. Every placed spot goes to Pipeline C research before it is applied. Existing spots to move in `existing_fixes`.

Fairness (`fairness.json`): `{ inside_destinations, standalone, new_destinations, lowest_kept, stronger_left_out: [{name, views, counted_sources, verdict, reason}], strongest_outsiders, benchmark }`. A stronger candidate left out without a concrete "What qualifies" reason fails the run.
- Destination shape (COVERAGE.md → "Prefer the region travellers name over one town in it", "A town is a spot only when the town itself is a draw", "Check neighbours before placing"): propose the NAMED REGION (a Wikivoyage region page or tourism brand) rather than one town whose draws are the neighbouring towns and parks; never list a town as a spot beside its own attraction (keep the attraction); and for every standalone spot within about 40 km of a proposed destination's centre, say in `placement.json` why it is not a spot of it.
