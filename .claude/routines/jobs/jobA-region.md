# Job: Pipeline A (coverage), enumerate + score + scripted filter, for ONE region
Repo: the repository root you were started in (edit NOTHING except curation/<branch>/region-<REGION>/candidates.json and verdicts.json). Roles: .claude/agents/coverage-enumerator.md and coverage-recall-checker.md — their checklists carry the rules this job needs; open COVERAGE.md only for a case they don't settle.

Region: <REGION> (its entry in scripts/coverage-regions.json; its land is the whole region, not its destinations).

0. LIST THE STRUCTURED SOURCES (script, no agent): `npx tsx scripts/curation/enumerate-structured.ts --region <REGION> --out curation/<branch>/region-<REGION>` reads Wikivoyage's See/Do entries (the region's `sources.wikivoyage` pages and the city and district pages they link to), the `sources.wikipedia_categories` and the OpenStreetMap attractions with a Wikipedia or Wikidata tag inside `sources.bbox`, and writes `candidates.structured.json` and `structured-report.json`. A region with no `sources` entry in `scripts/coverage-regions.json` needs the `--wikivoyage`, `--wikipedia-category` and `--bbox` flags (and should get a `sources` entry afterwards). Anything it lists as UNRESOLVED (a rate limit, an unreachable mirror) is retried with `--refresh`, never treated as empty.
1. ENUMERATE ADDITIONS (coverage-enumerator): only what the script could not read: the tourism board, agency and guidebook pages, local-language sources, unresolved sources, with each place's `wiki` title and sources, WITHOUT reading src/data/destinations.ts and WITHOUT redoing the structured list. Output `candidates.additions.json`. Fetch pages with `npx tsx scripts/curation/fetch.ts <url>` (shared cache). Then `npx tsx scripts/curation/enumerate-structured.ts --merge --out curation/<branch>/region-<REGION>` writes `candidates.json` from the two.
2. SCORE: `npx tsx scripts/curation/score-candidates.ts curation/<branch>/region-<REGION>/candidates.json` fills in views, coordinates, redirects, disambiguation pages and `existing_spot`. Fix by hand only what it reports it couldn't.
3. FILTER: `npx tsx scripts/curation/score-filter.ts --region-dir curation/<branch>/region-<REGION>` decides the clear cases by script and leaves `marginal.json` for the coverage-judge (jobA-judge.md). Existing spots in the region are included in it.
Final message: the filter's counts (auto-keep, auto-reject, marginal).
